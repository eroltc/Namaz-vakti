Rabbin icin (app.miqat.namaz)
Last updated / Son guncelleme: 5 September 2026 / 5 Eylul 2026

English

Who we are

Rabbin icin is a mobile app for Islamic prayer times, Qibla, azan reminders, Quran translation (meal), tasbih, Asma ul-Husna, and duas. It is published by the developer associated with GitHub eroltc.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not operate a user login.

  • Location: If you grant permission, the app uses your device location (or a location you set) to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location is processed on your device for these features.
  • Notifications & audio: With permission, the app may schedule local notifications and play short azan / reminder audio. Notification scheduling stays on the device.
  • Preferences: Settings (language, calculation method, location preference, etc.) are stored locally on your device.

We do not intentionally collect personal identity data (name, email, phone) through the app for accounts.

How data is used

Location and preferences are used only to provide prayer times, Qibla, notifications, and related in-app features. Data is stored locally on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your location or preferences with third parties for advertising. The app may rely on standard platform services provided by Google / Android for delivery of the app and notifications.

Data retention

Because data is stored locally, it remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Childrens privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The Last updated date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer via GitHub https://github.com/eroltc (open an issue on the app repository or use the profile contact methods available there).

Turkce

Kimiz

Rabbin icin, namaz vakitleri, kible, ezan hatirlatmalari, Kuran meali, tesbih, Esmaul-Husna ve dualar icin bir mobil uygulamadir. Gelistirici, GitHub eroltc hesabi ile iliskilidir.

Topladigimiz bilgiler

Uygulama hesap olusturmayi zorunlu kilmaz ve kullanici girisi isletmez.

  • Konum: Izin verirseniz uygulama, namaz vakitleri ve kible yonu hesaplamak icin cihaz konumunuzu (veya sizin sectiginiz konumu) kullanir. Konum bu ozellikler icin cihazinizda islenir.
  • Bildirimler ve ses: Izinle yerel bildirimler zamanlanabilir ve kisa ezan / hatirlatma sesi calinabilir. Bildirimler cihazda kalir.
  • Tercihler: Dil, hesaplama yontemi, konum tercihi vb. ayarlar yerel olarak cihazinizda saklanir.

Uygulama uzerinden kimlik bilgisi (ad, e-posta, telefon) ile hesap toplama amaclanmamaktadir.

Verilerin kullanimi

Konum ve tercihler yalnizca namaz vakitleri, kible, bildirimler ve ilgili uygulama ozelliklerini sunmak icin kullanilir. Veriler cihazinizda yerel olarak tutulur.

Paylasim

Kisisel verilerinizi satmayiz. Konumunuzu veya tercihlerinizi reklam amaciyla ucuncu taraflarla paylasmayiz. Uygulama dagitimi ve bildirimler icin Google / Android platform hizmetleri kullanilabilir.

Saklama

Veriler yerel oldugu icin uygulama verilerini temizleyene veya uygulamayi kaldirana kadar cihazinizda kalir.

Cocuklarin gizliligi

Uygulama genel kitleye uygundur; cocuklardan bilerek kisisel bilgi toplamaz.
